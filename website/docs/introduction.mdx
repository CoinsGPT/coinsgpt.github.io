# Introduction

![](/img/bitcoin/ChatBitcoinArch.png)

This architecture represents a **data-to-intelligence pipeline** where:

* Raw data → is unified (Data Layer)
* Structured into business logic (Semantic Layer)
* Enriched with reasoning and automation (Agentic Layer)
* And finally represented visually or conversationally (Representation Layer).

In essence, the system evolves from **data integration → semantic modeling → intelligent reasoning → user interaction**, forming the backbone of **AI-powered analytics and decision support**.


### 1. **Data Layer**

* **Purpose:** Collects and integrates data from multiple heterogeneous sources.
* **Sources shown:** ClickHouse and NebulaGraph.
* **Role:** Acts as the foundation of the system—aggregating and harmonizing data for further processing.

### 2. **Semantic Layer**

* **Purpose:** Defines business meaning and relationships of the raw data.
* **Function:** Converts technical data into a business-friendly model (metrics, KPIs, entities).
* **Outcome:** Provides a unified view of data semantics, enabling consistent understanding across analytics tools.

### 3. **Agentic Layer**

* **Purpose:** Adds intelligence, automation, and reasoning.
* **Function:** Uses AI agents or rules-based engines to interpret semantics, perform analysis, and execute autonomous data-driven tasks.
* **Outcome:** Enables proactive insights and contextual decision-making.

### 4. **Representation Layer**

* **Purpose:** Translates agentic insights into user-accessible formats.
* **Function:** Powers three main output channels:

  * **Embedded Analytics** – integrates analytics directly into applications.
  * **Dashboards & Reports** – visualizes structured insights for monitoring and analysis.
  * **Conversational Interface** – enables natural language interactions with data (e.g., chatbots, AI assistants).
